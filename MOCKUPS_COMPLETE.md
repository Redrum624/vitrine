## 🎯 Design Philosophy

### **1. Minimal Elegance: "Less is More"**
Inspired by Apple's design language. Every element serves a purpose. Whitespace is a feature, not wasted space. Creates a calm, zen-like editing environment that reduces cognitive load. Perfect for users who value simplicity and clarity.

### **2. Professional Studio: "Everything at Your Fingertips"**
Inspired by Capture One Pro and Adobe Lightroom. Power users want all tools visible without hunting through menus. Dense but meticulously organized layout optimizes professional workflows for maximum efficiency.

### **3. Modern Glass: "The Future of Editing"**
Contemporary aesthetic using cutting-edge glassmorphism design. Translucent panels, backdrop blur, and animated gradients create visual depth. Appeals to younger, design-conscious users who appreciate modern trends.
